LOCAL CRAFTSMANSHIP

Luxury Outdoor Spaces for Dover, MA Estates

Dover is renowned for its expansive private estates, wooded landscapes, and horses trails. Properties of this caliber demand outdoor spaces designed with architectural vision, high-grade structural engineering, and the finest finishes.

We design and build custom decks, multi-tiered platforms, and covered pavilions that integrate with Dover's wooded character. We pair premium polymer decking with custom glass or cable railings to maintain unobstructed views of your private grounds, creating a resort-like atmosphere.

E-E-A-T TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Navigating Dover Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Dover Building Department

Location: Town Hall, 5 Springdale Ave, Dover, MA 02030

Phone Contact: (508) 785-0032

Dover requires building permits and certified structural drawings for all new deck construction. Due to large minimum lot sizes (ranging from 1 to 2 acres), setback lines from property boundaries are significant and must be precisely surveyed. If your project falls near Dover's wooded wetlands or brooks, Conservation Commission approval is required.
