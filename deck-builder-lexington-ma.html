Custom Decks & Porches in Lexington, MA

Lexington features a unique mix of historic Colonial homes, mid-century modern residences, and custom new construction. Designing for these properties requires a versatile design-build process that adapts to different architectural eras and site layouts.

We specialize in premium materials, including Trex composite decking, TimberTech AZEK PVC polymer boards, and custom metal railings. These materials are built to withstand New England weather, maintaining their color and structural integrity without splintering, rotting, or fading.

We construct premium decks and screened porches designed for Lexington families. We choose durable composites and capped PVC boards that stand up to high-traffic, outdoor dining, and pets. Our hidden fasteners and low-voltage lighting create a clean, safe, and comfortable environment for evening gatherings.

PERMITS & REGULATIONS

Navigating Lexington Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Lexington Building Department

Location: Town Hall, 1625 Massachusetts Ave, Lexington, MA 02420

Phone Contact: (781) 698-4530

Lexington requires building permits for all decks, porches, and outdoor structures. The town enforces zoning bylaws that limit lot coverage and govern building setbacks. If your property is in a designated Historic District or within a 100-foot buffer zone of wetlands, you must obtain approval from the Historic Districts or Conservation Commission.
