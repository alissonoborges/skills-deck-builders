Navigating Newton Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Newton Inspectional Services Department

Location: Newton City Hall, 1000 Commonwealth Ave, Newton, MA 02459

Phone Contact: (617) 796-1200

In Newton, all deck constructions, structural alterations, and expansions require a building permit. The city enforces strict guidelines for maximum lot coverage, open space percentages, and setbacks. Decks within the 100-foot buffer zone of any wetland or bank must receive clearance from the Newton Conservation Commission.
