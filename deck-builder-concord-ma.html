Historic Context Deck Construction in Concord, MA

Concord is a community steeped in American history and natural beauty. Preserving its historic charm requires building methods that respect traditional styles while using durable, modern materials that perform well in the New England climate.

We specialize in premium materials, including Trex composite decking, TimberTech AZEK PVC polymer boards, and custom metal railings. These materials are built to withstand New England weather, maintaining their color and structural integrity without splintering, rotting, or fading.

We design custom decks, screened porches, and three-season outdoor rooms that blend with Concord's historic residences. We match the trim, paint colors, and structural details of your existing house, ensuring that the finished deck looks like it belongs to the original property.

PERMITS & REGULATIONS

Navigating Concord Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Concord Building Department

Location: 141 Keyes Rd, Concord, MA 01742

Phone Contact: (978) 318-3215

Concord enforces strict regulations on building permits, structural inspections, and setbacks. If your home is located within Concord's Historic Districts, the design must receive approval from the Historic Districts Commission. Concord's strict wetlands bylaws require filings with the Conservation Commission for any work near marshes, brooks, or rivers.
