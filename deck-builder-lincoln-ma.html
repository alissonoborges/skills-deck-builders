LOCAL CRAFTSMANSHIP

Custom Outdoor Spaces in Lincoln, MA

Lincoln is characterized by its rural integrity, historic preservation, and modernist residential architectures (including the historic Gropius House). Building in Lincoln requires a deep appreciation for geometric form, natural textures, and low-impact construction practices.

We specialize in modern and contemporary deck designs that respect Lincoln's wooded landscape. By combining clean-lined composite decking, dark aluminum rails, and integrated landscape lighting, we build spaces that blend into the canopy while offering a premium platform for outdoor living.

E-E-A-T TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Navigating Lincoln Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Lincoln Building Department

Location: Town Hall, 16 Lincoln Rd, Lincoln, MA 01773

Phone Contact: (781) 259-2613

Lincoln requires building permits for all structural deck projects. The town maintains strict bylaws regarding lot coverage, tree removal, and runoff management to preserve its rural character. Any work within 100 feet of wetlands or 200 feet of rivers must undergo review by the Lincoln Conservation Commission.
