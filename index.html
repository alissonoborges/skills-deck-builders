HOW WE WORK

A coordinated design-build process

Luxury is not only the final result. It is the experience from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

01

Initial Consultation

We visit your home to look at your yard, talk about what you want to build, and discuss how to make it work.

02

Design & Pricing

We sketch out a design, choose the right materials, and give you a clear, detailed estimate.

03

Zoning & Permit Approvals

We handle all the paperwork, submit architectural drawings to your town, and secure the building permits.

04

Selecting Your Materials

We help you choose the exact composite boards, railing colors, and lighting layout to match your house.

05

The Build

Our carpentry crew builds your deck. We keep the jobsite tidy, minimize noise, and update you daily on progress.

06

Final Walkthrough

We walk the deck with you to inspect every joint, railing, and stair light to ensure you're completely satisfied.
