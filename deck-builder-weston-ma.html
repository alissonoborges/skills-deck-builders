Navigating Weston Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Weston Building Department

Location: Town Hall, 11 Town House Rd, Weston, MA 02493

Phone Contact: (781) 786-5060

Weston requires structural plans and building permits for any deck, porch, or outdoor structure. The town's zoning regulations dictate strict lot coverage percentages and setbacks that vary by residential district (District A through D). In addition, projects near natural bodies of water or wetlands require filing with the Weston Conservation Commission.
