LOCAL CRAFTSMANSHIP

Custom Luxury Decks in Chestnut Hill, MA

Chestnut Hill is defined by its historic stone estates, refined brick residences, and sophisticated landscaping. Building in this neighborhood requires a design-first approach that respects the local character and blends new structures with established architectural facades.

Our custom deck packages focus on texture and material harmony. We pair warm, variegated composite decking (like Trex Transcend) with black powder-coated aluminum or stainless steel cable railings to create clean lines that preserve your property's historic charm while offering modern safety and structural reliability.

E-E-A-T TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Navigating Chestnut Hill Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Brookline / Newton Building Department

Location: Brookline Town Hall, 333 Washington St / Newton City Hall, 1000 Commonwealth Ave

Phone Contact: (617) 730-2100 / (617) 796-1200

Chestnut Hill lies within both Brookline and Newton. Depending on your property lines, plans must be submitted to either the Brookline Building Department or Newton Inspectional Services. Historically designated areas may require additional certificates of appropriateness from the local Historic Districts Commission.
