OUR STORY

Quality Carpentry, Built to Last

We are a dedicated design-build team specializing in custom composite decks and outdoor carpentry. We believe your backyard should feel like a natural, premium extension of your home.

OUR MISSION

Built with Integrity. Designed to Endure.

Skills Deck Builders was started with a simple idea: a deck should be built with the same level of care and precision as the inside of your home. We don't rush through generic builds or juggle dozens of projects at once. Instead, we focus on delivering premium, custom carpentry for homeowners who want quality over shortcuts.

Every project starts with a conversation about how you plan to use your space. We design decks that complement your home's original lines, handle Massachusetts weather, and look clean and solid for decades. We show up on time, keep the job site clean, and keep you updated every step of the way.

OUR VALUES

What Drives Us

Four principles guide every consultation, design decision and finished detail.

Intentionally Designed

We design every deck to match the architecture and layout of your house. We think about how you will move through the space, where the sun hits, and how the materials look together, starting from the first sketch.

Quality Carpentry

We take pride in our cuts, framing, and details. By using premium composite materials, clean hidden fasteners, and strong aluminum railing systems, we construct decks that stay safe, solid, and beautiful for years.

No Surprises

You will always know what is happening next. We give you a clear schedule, detailed pricing upfront, and daily updates while the crew is on site. We build trust through honest conversations and quick replies.

Respect for Your Yard

We treat your home like our own. We protect your lawn and gardens, keep the job site tidy, and clean up at the end of every day. Courteous builders and a clean workspace are non-negotiable.

OUR CREDENTIALS

Licensed. Insured. Trusted.

Professionalism is demonstrated, not declared. Here is what stands behind every project we deliver.

Massachusetts Construction License

CSL CS-119782 and HIC 207906. Fully licensed and registered in Massachusetts with complete insurance and jobsite accountability.

General Liability Insurance

Comprehensive liability coverage that protects your property and investment throughout the entire construction process, from site preparation to final walkthrough.

Workers' Compensation Coverage

Full workers' compensation insurance for every crew member on your property. Your peace of mind and our team's protection are equally important.

Material Certifications

We work exclusively with certified premium materials from industry-leading manufacturers. Every composite, hardwood and railing system meets or exceeds manufacturer specifications.

Building Code Compliance

Every structure is engineered and built to meet or exceed Massachusetts residential building codes, including load requirements, railing heights and structural framing standards.

Permit Assistance

We guide you through the municipal permit process, prepare necessary drawings and documentation, and coordinate with local building departments to ensure full compliance.
