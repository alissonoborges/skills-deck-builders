Urban Backyard Design & Construction in Brookline, MA

Brookline properties often feature compact, highly valuable backyard spaces and strict local historic preservation rules. Maximizing these environments requires a high level of design intelligence, custom engineering, and expertise in local municipal approvals.

We specialize in urban deck optimization, including multi-level layouts that provide separate dining and lounging zones, space-efficient staircases, and built-in planters. We utilize premium capped polymer materials that offer slip resistance and low maintenance, ideal for families and pet owners in Brookline.

Navigating Brookline Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Brookline Building Department

Location: Town Hall, 333 Washington St, Brookline, MA 02445

Phone Contact: (617) 730-2100

Brookline maintains detailed zoning regulations regarding side-yard and rear-yard setback lines, especially for high-density neighborhoods. Building permits are required for all decks. If your home falls within one of Brookline's Local Historic Districts, design plans must be approved by the Preservation Commission prior to filing.
