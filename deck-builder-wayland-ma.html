LOCAL CRAFTSMANSHIP

Custom & Poolside Decks in Wayland, MA

Wayland is defined by its quiet residential neighborhoods, wooded landscapes, and borders along the Sudbury River and Lake Cochituate. Creating outdoor spaces near these water features requires special materials and expert compliance with local environmental rules.

We design and build custom decks and poolside retreats that stand up to high moisture and constant sun. We select capped polymer PVC decking because it does not absorb water, resists mold and mildew, and offers a slip-resistant surface that stays comfortable for bare feet around pools.

E-E-A-T TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Navigating Wayland Permit Requirements

Obtaining correct town permits is crucial to protect your property's value and ensure structural safety. Our team handles the entire building permit process on your behalf, coordinating plans directly with local inspectors.

Building Department: Wayland Building Department

Location: Town Hall, 41 Cochituate Rd, Wayland, MA 01778

Phone Contact: (508) 358-3600

Wayland requires structural plans and building permits for all deck projects. The town maintains strict bylaws regarding lot coverage and setback lines. Due to Wayland's extensive watersheds and conservation land, any construction within 100 feet of wetlands or lakes must receive approval from the Wayland Conservation Commission.
