ENGINEERED PERFORMANCE

Why We Build with Premium Composites

Traditional wood decks look beautiful when first installed, but the harsh Massachusetts winter freezes and humid summers inevitably lead to splitting, warping, rot, and constant maintenance. Premium composite and capped PVC materials offer a better way forward.

These advanced materials combine the warm appearance of natural hardwoods with engineered cores that resist moisture penetration, UV fading, and insect damage. You get a beautiful, long-lasting deck that requires zero sanding, staining, or sealing.

INDUSTRY LEADERS

Comparing Trex, TimberTech, and Azek

Not all composite decking is created equal. We partner with the top three manufacturers in the industry to offer options that suit every design vision, budget, and environmental demand.

  • TimberTech AZEK (Capped Polymer PVC): The absolute pinnacle of low-maintenance decking. 100% polymer construction contains no wood fibers, making it exceptionally resistant to mold and moisture. Stays up to 30 degrees cooler in hot sun and offers a 50-year warranty.
  • Trex Transcend (Capped Composite): The pioneer of high-performance decking. Formulated with a heavy-duty, scratch-resistant outer shell and a core made of 95% recycled materials. Deep, realistic wood-grain patterns that won't fade or rot.
  • Multi-Tonal Colors: Authentic variegation replicates the character of fine hardwoods like Ipe, Tigerwood, and Teak.
