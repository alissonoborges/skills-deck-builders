LOCAL EXPERTISE

Navigating Building Departments & Zoning

Every town in Massachusetts has its own building codes, setback lines, lot coverage allowances, and conservation guidelines. We coordinate directly with local inspectors to ensure clean compliance.

Zoning Review

We analyze your property's lot size and zoning district to determine setbacks (distance from property lines) and maximum lot coverage. This keeps your project aligned with local bylaws from the start.

Conservation Commissions

If your property borders wetlands, ponds, or rivers, we manage filings under the Massachusetts Wetlands Protection Act, coordinating buffer-zone reviews and conservation site visits.

Structural Sign-off

Our drawings are prepared and engineered to comply with the 9th Edition of the Massachusetts State Building Code, guaranteeing that footings, beams, and joist spans are structurally verified.
